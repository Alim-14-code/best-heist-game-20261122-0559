A top-down 2D stealth heist game for the
[best-heist-game-20261122-0559](https://make.fun/bounty/best-heist-game-20261122-0559)
competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

You are a thief breaking into a night gallery to steal the Meridian Diamond
and escape through the green EXIT to the getaway van. Three guards patrol
with vision cones, two cameras sweep the halls, and the alarm meter decides
whether they hunt you. Tools: throw a coin to lure a guard away, or fire an
EMP to blind the cameras — both at a cost.

Controls: WASD or arrows to move, Shift to sneak, Q coin, E EMP, P pause,
Enter to start. No network, no libraries — plain canvas and WebAudio,
everything in `game/`.
